# Officer Handoff Checklist

GSSA Bylaws Art. IX §9.8: *"Outgoing officers shall transfer access to digital
accounts and assets within ten business days after their terms end and shall not
retain or use access after the transfer."*

Terms end **April 30**. New officers take office **May 1**. This checklist
satisfies the bylaw for the website.

---

## The rule that matters most

**Nothing here may live in one person's personal account.** Bylaws Art. IX §9.7
requires organization-controlled contact information and recovery methods.

The GitHub organization must have:

- at least **two** officers with Owner permission at all times
- a **GSSA-owned recovery email**, never an individual's personal address
- the graduating officers removed once the new board can sign in

If only one person can administer the site, the organization is one graduation
away from losing it.

---

## Outgoing officers — before April 30

- [ ] Add every incoming officer to the **gssa-slu** GitHub organization
- [ ] Grant at least two of them **Owner** permission, not just Member
- [ ] Walk one incoming officer through publishing a news item, start to finish,
      while you are still available to help
- [ ] Confirm the organization's recovery email is a GSSA address
- [ ] Add board entries for the incoming officers (see below)
- [ ] Hand over any social media credentials referenced in Site Settings

## Incoming officers — first week of May

- [ ] Sign in at [app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org) and confirm you can
      **save** a change, not just view one
- [ ] In the editor, open **Board Members** and add one entry per new officer:
      - set **Term** to the new year, e.g. `2027-2028`
      - fill in name, program, bio, photo, pronunciation, email, office hours
- [ ] Open **Site Settings** and change **Current term** to the same value
- [ ] Check the Executive Board page: the new board should be at the top, and
      last year's should have moved into **Past Boards** on its own
- [ ] Update **Site Settings → Contact email** if the role addresses changed
- [ ] Post a news item introducing the new board

## Then, and only then

- [ ] Remove the graduating officers from the GitHub organization
- [ ] Confirm the site still builds — make any small edit and watch it go live

---

## Do not

- **Do not delete past officers.** The archive is GSSA's public record of who
  served. Changing the term is what moves them; deleting destroys the record.
- **Do not transfer the repository to a personal account.** It stops being an
  organizational asset the moment you do.
- **Do not put roster, minutes, applications or budgets in the repository.** It
  is public. Bylaws Art. IX §9.3.

## If something goes wrong

Every save is a commit. Any previous version of any page can be restored from the
repository's history, including work from officers who left years ago. Nothing
done through the editor is unrecoverable.

If nobody can sign in at all, GitHub account recovery runs through the
organization's recovery email — which is why the first checklist item exists.
