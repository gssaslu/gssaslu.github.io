  We're excited to invite you to **GSSA's first GIS Day celebration on
  Wednesday, November 18, from 11 AM to 4 PM at SLU's Center for Global
  Citizenship!** 🎉


  We've planned a fun day with GeoGuessr, career conversations with geospatial
  professionals, creative mapping activities, free pizza, and a *Paint the Cup*
  activity in collaboration with the Graduate Student Association (GSA)!


  **🗺️ Creative Map Challenge: Make a Map, Win a Prize!**


  We're inviting students to submit their own creative maps for our GIS Day Map
  Challenge!


  Your map can be about anything you find interesting. It doesn't have to be
  research-related or particularly technical. Have fun with it and get creative!


  We'll showcase the maps during GIS Day, and attendees will vote for their
  favorites. **The winning map will receive a prize! 🎁**


  **We'll share the submission form and more later this week. In the meantime,
  get started on your maps!**
